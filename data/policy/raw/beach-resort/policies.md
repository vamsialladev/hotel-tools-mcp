# Beach Resort - Property Policies

## Pets
Dogs are welcome up to 40 lbs, maximum 2 dogs per room. Pet fee is $75 per stay (non-refundable). Dogs are not allowed at the pool or in the restaurant. Service animals are always welcome with no fee.

## Pool and Beach
The rooftop pool is open 7:00 AM to 10:00 PM. Guests under 16 must be with an adult.
Beach chair set (2 chairs + umbrella): $40 per day, book with the concierge.

## Parking
Valet parking only, $45 per night with in/out privileges. Two EV charging spots, first come first served.

## Airport Transfers
Pickup from MIA: sedan $65 one way (up to 3 guests), SUV $95 one way (up to 6 guests). Book at least 24 hours in advance.
We do not offer transfers from Fort Lauderdale (FLL). Recommend a taxi or rideshare.

## Late Check-out (Property Override)
Because of high weekend occupancy, late check-out on Saturdays and Sundays is capped at 12:00 PM for all guests, including Silver and Gold members. Platinum members may check out until 2:00 PM on weekends.

## Resort Fee
$35 per night plus tax. Includes Wi-Fi, bike rental and two bottles of water per day.
