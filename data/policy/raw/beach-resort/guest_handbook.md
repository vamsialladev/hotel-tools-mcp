# Guest Handbook - Beach Resort

## Wi-Fi
Wi-Fi is included in the resort fee. Network: BeachResort-Guest. The password is printed on the key card sleeve. Coverage includes all rooms, the rooftop pool and the beach chair area.

## Early Check-in
Early check-in from 12:00 PM costs $30 if the room is ready. Guaranteed early check-in is not offered. Platinum members get early check-in from 1:00 PM at no charge, subject to availability.

## Fitness Center
The fitness center (gym) on level 2 is open 24 hours with your room key. Guests under 16 must be with an adult. Towels and water are provided.

## Laundry
Same-day valet laundry and dry cleaning: drop items at the front desk by 9:00 AM for return by 6:00 PM. Prices are on the laundry card in the room. There is no self-service laundry room.

## Quiet Hours
Quiet hours are 11:00 PM to 7:00 AM. Music at the rooftop pool ends when the pool closes at 10:00 PM.

## Room Service
In-room dining is available 6:30 AM to 11:00 PM from the Main Restaurant menu. A $7 delivery fee plus 18% gratuity is added to each order.
