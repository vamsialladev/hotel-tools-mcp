# Guest Handbook - Mountain Lodge

## Wi-Fi
Wi-Fi is free for all guests (there is no resort fee). Network: MountainLodge-Guest. The password is printed on the key card sleeve. The signal is weaker in the cabins; a free range extender is available at the front desk.

## Breakfast
A continental breakfast is included in every room rate, served 7:00 AM to 10:00 AM in the Great Room. Mountain Lodge has no restaurant or room service; guests may have food delivered from local restaurants to the front desk.

## Laundry
A guest coin laundry on the ground floor is open 7:00 AM to 10:00 PM. Detergent pods are $2 at the front desk.

## Hiking and Activities
Free trail maps are at the front desk; the Ridge Loop trail (3 miles) starts behind the lodge. Guided hikes run Saturdays at 9:00 AM for $20 per person; book at the front desk by Friday evening.
