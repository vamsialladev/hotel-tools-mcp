# Mountain Lodge - Property Policies

## Pets
We are dog-friendly: dogs of any size, maximum 2 per room. Pet fee is $50 per night per room, capped at $200 per stay. Dog beds and bowls available on request. Cats and other pets are not permitted.

## Amenities
Mountain Lodge does not have an on-site pool. Guests receive free day passes to a partner fitness center 0.5 miles away, which has an indoor pool.
Fire pit evenings run 6:00 PM to 10:00 PM. S'mores kits are $12 at the front desk.

## Parking
Free self-parking. RVs and trailers must use the overflow lot - ask the front desk for directions.

## Airport Transfers
Shuttle to and from Asheville Regional Airport (AVL): $35 per person one way. Runs hourly on the hour from 7:00 AM to 9:00 PM. Book at least 12 hours ahead.

## Quiet Hours
Quiet hours are 10:00 PM to 7:00 AM. After a second noise complaint, the Manager on Duty visits the room. After a third complaint, the guest may be asked to leave without a refund (Manager on Duty decision).
