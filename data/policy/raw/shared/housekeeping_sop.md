# Housekeeping SOP

## Daily Service
Rooms are serviced daily between 9:00 AM and 4:00 PM. Guests can opt out of daily service; opting out earns 250 Guest Rewards points per night.

## Extra Items
Extra towels, pillows and toiletries are free and delivered within 20 minutes. Rollaway beds are $25 per night and depend on room size. Cribs are free.

## Lost and Found
Items are logged in the Lost & Found binder and kept for 90 days. Shipping to the guest is at the guest's cost. Never ship items without a confirmed address from the guest's own email.
