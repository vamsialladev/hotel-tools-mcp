# Guest Rewards Loyalty Program

## Tiers and Benefits
| Tier | Nights per year | Late check-out | Welcome amenity | Room upgrade |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Silver | 5+ | 1:00 PM (free) | Bottled water | No |
| Gold | 15+ | 2:00 PM (free) | Drink voucher | Subject to availability |
| Platinum | 40+ | 4:00 PM (guaranteed) | Bottle of wine | Guaranteed one category |

## Earning Points
Members earn 10 points per US dollar spent on the room rate. Taxes, resort fees and pet fees do not earn points.

## Verifying Membership
Always confirm the member number in the guest's PMS profile. Never grant tier benefits based on a verbal claim alone.
