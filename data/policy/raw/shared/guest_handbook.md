# Hotel Group Guest Handbook

These standards apply to every property in the group unless a property policy says otherwise.

## Early Check-in
Early check-in before the standard 3:00 PM depends on which rooms are ready and is never guaranteed at booking. Each property sets its own early check-in fee and earliest time.

## Luggage Storage
The front desk stores luggage free of charge before check-in and after check-out on the same day. Bags are tagged and kept in a locked room behind the front desk. Luggage cannot be stored overnight after check-out.

## Accessibility
Every property has accessible rooms with roll-in showers, grab bars and lowered closet rails. Visual alarm and doorbell kits are available on request. Ask for an accessible room when booking; the front desk confirms it at check-in. Service animals are always welcome.

## Wake-up Calls and Deliveries
The front desk sets wake-up calls on request. Packages for guests are held at the front desk for up to 7 days.
