# Hotel Group Brand Standards

These standards apply to every property in the group unless a property policy says otherwise.

## Check-in and Check-out
Standard check-in is 3:00 PM. Standard check-out is 11:00 AM.

## Late Check-out
Late check-out is always subject to availability. Guests who are not loyalty members may request late check-out until 1:00 PM for a $30 fee. Loyalty members receive late check-out according to their tier (see Guest Rewards program). Individual properties may set stricter limits.

## Cancellation
Flexible rate: free cancellation up to 48 hours before arrival. Cancellations inside 48 hours are charged one night plus tax.
Non-refundable rate: no refunds or date changes under any circumstances, except when approved by the General Manager.

## Smoking
All properties in the group are 100% non-smoking, including balconies and patios. A $250 cleaning fee is charged when there is evidence of smoking in a room.

## Refunds and Escalation
Front desk agents may issue courtesy credits up to $50 without approval. Any refund or credit over $200 must be approved by the Manager on Duty.
